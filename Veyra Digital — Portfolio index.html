Graphic design

Wordmarks, visual identity, and the supporting assets a brand needs beyond its website.

Good design decisions are usually the quiet ones — a paragraph a few pixels narrower than it needs to be, a button that moves like flesh instead of plastic. I care more about whether a site holds together under scrutiny than whether it looks striking in a screenshot.

That means clear hierarchy before decoration, layouts that hold up on a phone in bad light, animation that earns its keep, and the restraint to leave a page alone once it's right.

About

I'm the person behind Veyra Digital. I design and build websites — mostly for restaurants, hospitality brands, and independent labels that need a site to carry the same level of care as everything else they make.

My process starts with the content and the audience, not a template: what does this business need visitors to understand in the first ten seconds, and what's the shortest path from landing to that understanding? Everything after — palette, type, motion — follows from that.

On the development side, I care about the site working as well as it looks: fast loads, layouts that hold up across devices, and code that isn't held together with duct tape.
